FAQ

Good questions.

Will it actually go off if my phone's on silent or in a Focus mode?
Yes. Rise schedules real system alarms through Apple's AlarmKit — the same category the built-in Clock app uses. They ring through Silent Mode, Do Not Disturb, and every Focus.
What happens if I force-quit the app?
The alarm still rings. It's scheduled at the OS level, not something Rise's own process has to stay alive for in the background.
Can I still snooze?
Once, for five minutes, per alarm. After that snooze is used, the alarm keeps ringing until you finish the wake-up task — no infinite snoozing.
Is the app locking a real block, or just a screen I can swipe past?
It's built on Apple's actual Screen Time / Family Controls APIs — the same system parental controls use. There's no in-app way around it; the only way out is finishing your checklist or waiting out the timer.
What platforms is Rise on?
iOS, built specifically around AlarmKit and Screen Time — the two things that make the alarm and the app-locking genuinely real. That focus means no Android version for now.
What do I need to get started?
An iPhone on iOS 26 or later. Download Rise, allow the alarm and Screen Time permissions when it asks, and set your first alarm — that's the whole setup.
